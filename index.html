Today, we join forces to serve even bigger events when the event calls for it. FAQ

Q: How long does the facepaint stay on?

A: Our professional face paint stays on as long as you keep it away from water.

Q: What services do you offer?

A: We offer face painting for all your fun events and needs!

Q: Is there a minimum deposit?

A: We do not require a minimum deposit. You have time to pay up until the conclusion of the event!

Q: Do you have travel fees or other fees?

A: We have absolutely no hidden fees. It is the flat rate of $125 per hour. A discount is provided to those who book 3 or more hours.

Q: Do you do themed parties?

A: For no extra charge, we accommodate the majority of themes. After you submit the booking form, we will contact you to work out the details.

Q: What areas do you service?

A: We go an hour and a half from Leesburg, FL in any direction. Book an event today!

Q: How large of an event can you accommodate for?

A: The Glitter Gecko has accommodated for events as small as 10 party goers and as large as 10000+ attendees. We have a network of facepainters that could accommodate any size event.
